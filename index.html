Women for women · Est. 2020

Every woman in Israel should feel safe and protected.

We are here for HER.

HER Israel brings women together, new olot, female lone soldiers, students, professionals, and immigrant mothers, through community programs, networking, career panels, mentorship, and real, practical support.

01 About

Making aliyah and moving to a new country, serving in the IDF far from family, or starting a career without speaking the language, it’s easier with women beside you. HER Israel connects you to a real community and to the support you need.
